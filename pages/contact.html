Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Islam ?

Islam is submitting to Allah by believing only in Him (tawhid), obeying Him, and following the commands of His Messenger Muhammad doing what he ordered and avoiding what he forbade.

Who is the sahaba ?

The sahaba are the Muslims who saw the Prophet, believed in him and his message, and died as Muslims.

Why does Islam influence non-Muslims and turn them to Islam?

Most people who accept Islam do so because they find answers to their unanswered questions in Islam, and over time they come to see it as the right path and find peace in Allah's mercy.
One example is Dr. Jeffrey Lang becoming a Muslim, and you can find a lecture about why he chose Islam called

Does Islam call for violence? Does it call for killing or forcing others into it ?

Islam does not encourage compulsion in religion; rather, Allah says, "There is no compulsion in religion." But the image most non-Muslims commonly have is incorrect.
