Resources

The website draws exclusively from the following authenticated Islamic sources:

  • The Holy Qur'an — The primary and highest source of Islamic knowledge.
  • Sahih al-Bukhari — Compiled by Imam Muhammad ibn Ismail al-Bukhari. One of the two most authentic hadith collections in Sunni Islam.
  • Sahih Muslim — Compiled by Imam Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj. Regarded alongside Sahih al-Bukhari as the most reliable hadith reference.
  • Al-Bidayah wa'l-Nihayah — Ibn Kathir — A comprehensive historical and Islamic chronicle by the renowned scholar Ismail ibn Kathir.
  • Hayat as-Sahabah — Muhammad Yusuf al-Kandhlawi — A major reference for the biographies and stories of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Riyad as-Salihin — Imam al-Nawawi — A classic collection of hadith compiled by Imam Yahya ibn Sharaf al-Nawawi.
  • Fiqh as-Sunnah — Sayyid Sabiq — A widely used reference in Islamic jurisprudence, grounding rulings in Qur'anic verses and authenticated hadith.
