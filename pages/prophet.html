Sira Highlight

Birth

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born in Makkah into the noble clan of Banu Hashim. His father, Abdullah, died before his birth, and his mother, Aminah, passed away when he was still a child. He was cared for by his grandfather and later his uncle Abu Talib. As he grew, he became known for honesty, trustworthiness, and noble character, earning the title Al-Amin.

Before The Beginning

Before revelation, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ lived a life marked by integrity and reflection. He worked as a shepherd and later became a respected merchant. His honesty and character were widely recognized, and he married Khadijah, with whom he shared a life of devotion and trust. As he approached forty, he increasingly withdrew to the Cave of Hira, seeking solitude and worship.

The Beginning

At the age of forty, the first revelation came to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ through Jibril in the Cave of Hira, beginning with the command to read. Revelation continued, calling humanity to worship Allah alone, abandon idolatry, uphold justice, and live with righteousness. He first called those closest to him, then proclaimed the message openly. The early Muslims endured rejection, ridicule, and severe persecution.

The Hijrah

As opposition in Makkah intensified, some Muslims migrated to Abyssinia, while others endured hardship at home. Eventually, Allah permitted the Prophet ﷺ and the Muslims to migrate to Madinah. The Hijrah marked a decisive turning point: faith was no longer merely defended through patience under persecution, but became the foundation of a growing community. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ arrived in Madinah and began building that community upon brotherhood, worship, justice, and mutual responsibility.

Leadership And Victory From Allah

In Madinah, the Prophet ﷺ established a community that united the Muhajirun and the Ansar and developed a society governed by faith, justice, and responsibility. The Muslims faced major trials, including Badr, Uhud, and the Battle of the Trench, while treaties and changing alliances shaped the region. Through these years, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ demonstrated leadership in worship, family life, diplomacy, conflict, forgiveness, and concern for the vulnerable.

The Beginning After The End

In the final years, the Prophet ﷺ returned to Makkah, where the city came under Muslim authority, and he emphasized mercy rather than vengeance. He performed his Farewell Hajj and reminded the Muslims of fundamental principles of faith, justice, dignity, and responsibility. He ﷺ passed away in Madinah at the age of sixty-three. His life remains a lasting example of worship, character, patience, leadership, and devotion to Allah. Until we meet Him on the Day of Resurrection.
